name, subtitle, icon and screenshots' headline art: both stores reject
apps that look like they come from another brand. Mentioning that stickers
work with WhatsApp in the description is fine.

## Description (App Store and Play)

Peel turns your photos and videos into stickers in a few taps.

Pick a photo and Peel cuts out the person, pet or thing in it, adds a
white sticker edge, and it's ready. Pick a video and Peel makes a moving
sticker from the best few seconds.

• Automatic cut-outs, made on your phone
• Moving stickers from any video
• Add text, in two sizes
• Add a sound: record your voice or pick an audio file
• Keep stickers in packs and name them
• Add packs to WhatsApp in one tap
• Copy a sticker and paste it straight into a chat
• Light and dark mode

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your photos never
leave your phone.

Peel works with WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business. Peel isn't made by or
affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

## App Store review notes

Peel makes stickers on the device; there's no account or login.
To try it: tap the photo or video card, pick something, then Save sticker.
"Add to WhatsApp" needs WhatsApp installed. Without it, Save to Photos,
Share and Copy still work. Moving stickers copy as a GIF.

## Data safety (Play)

Peel's own code collects and shares nothing. Google ML Kit (used for
cut-outs) collects some diagnostics. Check the live
[ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure)
before submitting; at the time of writing it means:

- Does your app collect or share user data? **Yes** (ML Kit diagnostics).
- Data types collected: **App info and performance → Diagnostics**, and
  **Device or other IDs** (ML Kit uses Firebase Installations).
- Shared with third parties: **No**.
- Processed ephemerally: No. Required, not optional: Yes.
- Purpose: **Analytics** (diagnostics).
- Encrypted in transit: **Yes**.
- Users can request deletion: No (no account; nothing is tied to a person).
- Photos, videos, audio files, voice recordings: **not collected**; they
  stay on the device.
