Peel privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Peel turns your photos and videos into stickers. It's built to work without knowing anything about you.

What Peel does with your photos, videos and sounds

Everything happens on your phone. Peel cuts out, trims and compresses your stickers on the device. The photos, videos and sounds you pick or record are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Your stickers are stored inside Peel on your phone. Deleting a sticker or the app removes them.

No account, no tracking

There's no sign-up, no profile, no advertising and no analytics in Peel. We don't track you across apps or websites.

What leaves your phone

Only what you choose to send:

  • stickers you add to WhatsApp, which WhatsApp then handles under its own privacy policy;
  • files you save to Photos or share to another app.

Permissions

  • Photos: to pick the photo or video you want to turn into a sticker, and to save stickers when you ask.
  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo.
  • Microphone: only while you record a sound for a sticker.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings.

Android: Google ML Kit

On Android, the cut-out uses Google's ML Kit, which runs on the phone. Google's ML Kit may send Google technical information to keep it working, such as the device model, Android version and how the feature performed. It doesn't include your photos. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

On iPhone, the cut-out uses Apple's on-device Vision framework and sends nothing.

Children

Peel isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
