Questions.

Is Peel free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions and no sign-up.

Is it safe to install the APK?

Yes. Peel is open source, so anyone can read the code on GitHub. Android warns about every app from outside Google Play, so the warning is normal.

Does Peel upload my photos?

No. Cut-outs, trimming and compression happen on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there’s no account.

Can stickers have sound?

Yes. Record your voice or pick an audio file. Peel saves a short clip, and you finish it in WhatsApp’s own Create sticker.
